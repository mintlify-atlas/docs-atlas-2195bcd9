The Claims Management System is a backend REST API built with Spring Boot for telecommunications companies to manage service incidents end-to-end. It automates claim registration, routes incidents to the right technical employee using business rules, tracks every status change with a full audit trail, and supports claim transfers between specialists with complete traceability.

## How it works

The system models the complete journey of a telecom service incident through four stages: registration, assignment, transfer (optional), and resolution.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register a claim">
    A commercial employee logs an incident by identifying the client, affected service contract, claim type, and urgency classification (Urgent or Critical).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic assignment">
    The assignment engine selects the best technical employee based on the classification: the least-loaded expert for Urgent claims, or the most experienced expert for Critical claims.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer if needed">
    A technical employee can transfer a claim to another qualified expert, recording the motivation and updating the audit history automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve the claim">
    The assigned technical employee records the solution description. The system sets the claim to Resolved, logs the resolution date, and links the solution to the audit history.
  </Step>
</Steps>
