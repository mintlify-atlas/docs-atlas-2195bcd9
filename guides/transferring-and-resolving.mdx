---
title: "How to transfer and resolve claims as a Technical Employee"
description: "Guide for Technical Employees: transfer a claim to another expert with a stated motivation, or record a solution to set the claim status to Resuelto."
---

Technical Employees are responsible for resolving assigned claims. When a claim falls outside your area of expertise, you can transfer it to a more suitable colleague. When you have a fix, you record the solution and the system finalizes the claim.

<Note>
You must be authenticated with the `TECHNICAL_EMPLOYEE` role. You can only act on claims in your own assigned list.
</Note>

## Transfer a claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit the transfer">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/claims/claim-transfer \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "idClaim": 5,
        "motivation": "Requires fiber optic specialist"
      }'
    ```
    The system re-runs the assignment engine (excluding you) and assigns the next eligible technician. A `Transfer` record and a `ClaimHistory` entry are both created automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
You can only transfer claims currently in your assigned list. Attempting to transfer a claim not in your queue will result in an error response.
</Warning>

## Resolve a claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Record the solution">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/claims/resolved-claim \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "idClaim": 5,
        "description": "Replaced damaged cable segment at junction box B-14. Service restored."
      }'
    ```
    The system sets `currentStatus` to `"Resuelto"`, creates a `Solution` entity with the current timestamp, and appends a final `ClaimHistory` entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Resolution is final. Once a claim is resolved it cannot be re-opened through the API.
</Note>

## Check your assigned claims

```bash
curl http://localhost:8080/api/TechnicalEmployee/7/claims-assigned \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>"
```

Returns a list of all pending claims assigned to that technician. Requires `ADMIN` or `TECHNICAL_EMPLOYEE` role.

</Tip>
